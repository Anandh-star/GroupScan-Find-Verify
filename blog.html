The "₹100 Mobile Phone" Scam on Instagram & FB

You are scrolling through Instagram and see a sponsored ad: "Flipkart Warehouse Clearance! iPhone for just ₹99!" The link takes you to a website that looks exactly like Amazon or Flipkart.

How they trap you: They ask for ₹99 via UPI. Once you enter your UPI PIN, they might hack your device or deduct a much larger amount. They will never deliver a phone.

💡 Fact: No company sells a ₹50,000 phone for ₹100. If it's too good to be true, it is a scam.

Telegram Investment & Crypto Groups

You join a Telegram group with 50,000 members. People are constantly posting screenshots of receiving huge profits. The admin says, "Send ₹5,000, I will trade for you and return ₹15,000 in 2 hours."

The Reality Check: Those "happy members" are bots or fake accounts managed by the scammer. Once you send the money via UPI or Crypto, the admin will block you and delete the chat history.

💡 Fact: Never send money to an unknown "Trader" or "Admin" on Telegram.

WhatsApp "Like YouTube Videos" Task Scam

An unknown number messages you on WhatsApp offering a part-time job. "Just like 3 YouTube videos and get ₹150 instantly." They actually pay you ₹150 for the first task to gain your trust.

The Trap: Next, they add you to a "Premium Telegram Group" and ask you to invest ₹5,000 for "Prepaid VIP tasks" to earn ₹10,000. You pay, and they disappear with your money.

💡 Fact: Legitimate companies do not hire people via random WhatsApp messages to like videos.

Your Digital Armor 🛡️

🔗

Check the URL carefully

Scammers use domains like 'amazon-offer-sale.in'. Always verify if it is the official website (amazon.in, flipkart.com) before making any payment.

🛑

UPI PIN means Money OUT

You only need to enter your UPI PIN when you are sending money. You NEVER need to enter a PIN to receive money or gifts.
